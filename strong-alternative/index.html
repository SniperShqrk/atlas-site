A Strong alternative that reads your training

Strong logs your sets well. ATLAS logs them too, then reads what you logged: which muscles are recovered, which you are neglecting and which lifts have stalled.

What ATLAS adds to a log

  • A diagnosis in plain sentences. A neglected muscle, push outrunning pull, a lift that has stopped moving, each with the numbers behind it.
  • A recovery map. Every muscle shaded by the sets you logged, so today goes where it does the most.
  • Last session filled in. Every set shows what you did last time and the step that beats it.
  • An AI planner and coach. The planner (ATLAS PRO) builds a program around your days, time, equipment and history. The coach is free to try with 10 messages, with about 250 a month in ATLAS PRO, shared with the planner and program import, and answers questions about your own training and proposes exact changes; you apply them with a tap and every change can be undone.

Bring your Strong history

In Strong, open Settings and choose Export data, then import the CSV in ATLAS. Every workout lands in your history and counts toward records, the recovery map and the diagnosis, as if you had logged it in ATLAS. The file is read on your phone and you see a preview before anything is imported. Import is free. Step by step: importing from Strong or Hevy.

What it costs

Logging, history, the recovery map, records and CSV export are free, with no session caps and no trial. ATLAS PRO is EUR 4.99 a month or EUR 29.99 a year; your store shows the price in your currency.

What ATLAS does not do

No nutrition database, no wearable readiness scores, no voice logging, no public leaderboards and no social feed. Accounts are for backup only. ATLAS is also new: the iPhone app is the first to open to everyone and the Android app is still in testing, so if a store page does not open for you yet, keep using Strong until it does.

Questions

Can I move my Strong workouts to ATLAS?

Yes. Export your data from Strong as a CSV file and import it from Profile in ATLAS. Strong's current and older export formats are read, kg and lb. It is free.

Is ATLAS free?

Logging, history, the recovery map, records and CSV export are free, with no session caps and no trial. ATLAS PRO is EUR 4.99 a month or EUR 29.99 a year; your store shows the price in your currency.

Should I switch from Strong?

If you only want to record sets, either app does it. Switch if you want your log read back to you: what to change, what is recovered and what has stalled. You can import your Strong history and compare for yourself.
