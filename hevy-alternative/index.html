A Hevy alternative with a diagnosis

Hevy records your workouts. ATLAS records them and then tells you what they add up to: recovered muscles, neglected ones and lifts that have stalled.

What ATLAS adds to a log

  • A diagnosis in plain sentences. A neglected muscle, push outrunning pull, a lift that has stopped moving, each with the numbers behind it.
  • A recovery map. Every muscle shaded by the sets you logged, so today goes where it does the most.
  • Last session filled in. Every set shows what you did last time and the step that beats it.
  • An AI planner and coach (ATLAS PRO). The planner builds a program around your days, time, equipment and history. The coach answers questions about your own training and proposes exact changes; you apply them with a tap and every change can be undone.

Bring your Hevy history

In Hevy, open Settings, then Export & import data, and export your workouts as a CSV; import that file in ATLAS. Every workout lands in your history and counts toward records, the recovery map and the diagnosis, as if you had logged it in ATLAS. The file is read on your phone and you see a preview before anything is imported. Import is free. Step by step: importing from Strong or Hevy.

What it costs

Logging, history, the recovery map, records and CSV export are free, with no session caps and no trial. ATLAS PRO is EUR 4.99 a month or EUR 29.99 a year; your store shows the price in your currency.

What ATLAS does not do

No nutrition database, no wearable readiness scores, no voice logging, no public leaderboards and no social feed. Accounts are for backup only. ATLAS is also new: the iPhone app is the first to open to everyone and the Android app is still in testing, so if a store page does not open for you yet, keep using Hevy until it does.

Questions

Can I move my Hevy workouts to ATLAS?

Yes. Export your workouts from Hevy as a CSV file and import it from Profile in ATLAS. Hevy exports in kg and lb are read. It is free.

Is ATLAS free?

Logging, history, the recovery map, records and CSV export are free, with no session caps and no trial. ATLAS PRO is EUR 4.99 a month or EUR 29.99 a year; your store shows the price in your currency.

Does ATLAS have a social feed?

No. ATLAS has no social feed, friends or public leaderboards. An account is only there to back up and sync your training.
