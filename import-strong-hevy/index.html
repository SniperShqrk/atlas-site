Import guide

Import your Strong or Hevy history

Bring your training with you. Every workout lands in your ATLAS history and counts toward records, the recovery map and the diagnosis, as if you had logged it there.

Before you start

You need the ATLAS app on your phone and a CSV export from Strong or Hevy. Import is free. The file is read on your phone, you see a preview before anything is imported, and the whole import can be undone.

Steps

  1. Export from Strong. Open Settings and choose Export data. Save the CSV file to Files.
  2. Or export from Hevy. Open Settings, then Export & import data, and export your workouts. Save the CSV file to Files.
  3. Open ATLAS. Go to Profile and tap Import from Strong / Hevy.
  4. Choose the file. Tap Choose CSV file and pick the one you saved.
  5. Check the preview. ATLAS shows what it found before anything is imported. Confirm to add the workouts to your history.
  6. Changed your mind? Tap Undo this import and the workouts it added are removed.

What is read

Strong's current export and its older formats, and Hevy exports in kg or lb. Files re-saved in a spreadsheet are usually fine too. If a row cannot be read, the preview tells you how many were skipped.

After the import

Your past workouts feed the recovery map and the diagnosis from the first day, so ATLAS can already tell you what is neglected and what has stalled. Last session is filled in on every lift, with the step to beat it.

Questions

Is importing from Strong or Hevy free?

Yes. Import and CSV export are free for everyone, with no session caps and no trial.

Is my file uploaded anywhere?

The file is read on your phone. Your workouts are stored on your device, and are backed up to your account only if you sign in.

Can I undo an import?

Yes. After importing, Undo this import removes the workouts it added.

Get the app, then import.

iPhone first, then Android. No trial, no ads.
