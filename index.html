Questions
Is ATLAS free?

Yes. Logging, history, the recovery map, records, import and export are free with no session caps and no trial. The AI coach is free to try with 10 messages. ATLAS PRO adds the full diagnosis, the AI planner, about 250 coach messages a month, shared with the planner and program import, and strength charts.

Where can I download it?

Search for ATLAS in the App Store on iPhone or Google Play on Android, or use the buttons on this page. The iPhone app is the first to open to everyone; the Android app is in testing now.

Can I bring my Strong or Hevy history?

Yes. Export a CSV from Strong or Hevy and import it in ATLAS. Your past workouts feed the recovery map and the diagnosis from day one.

Do I need an account?

Not to use the app, but it is strongly recommended. Without one your training lives only on your phone and is not backed up: lose or replace the phone and it is gone. With an account it syncs to the cloud.

How is ATLAS different from Strong or Hevy?

All three log sets well. ATLAS then does the thinking: it reads your log and tells you which muscles are recovered, which you are neglecting, where push and pull are out of balance and which lifts have stalled. The AI planner, part of ATLAS PRO, builds a program around your days, time, equipment and history. The AI coach, free to try with 10 messages and about 250 a month in ATLAS PRO, answers questions about your own training and proposes exact changes to your plan. You apply them with a tap, and every change can be undone.
