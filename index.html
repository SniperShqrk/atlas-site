Questions
Is ATLAS free?

Yes. Logging, history, the recovery map, records, import and export are free with no session caps and no trial. ATLAS PRO adds the full diagnosis, the AI planner, the AI coach and strength charts.

When can I download it?

iPhone first: it is the closest to launch. Android follows; it is in testing on Google Play now. Leave your email and you get one message when each one lands.

Can I bring my Strong or Hevy history?

Yes. Export a CSV from Strong or Hevy and import it in ATLAS. Your past workouts feed the recovery map and the diagnosis from day one.

Do I need an account?

Not to use the app, but it is strongly recommended. Without one your training lives only on your phone and is not backed up: lose or replace the phone and it is gone. With an account it syncs to the cloud.

How is ATLAS different from Strong or Hevy?

All three log sets well. ATLAS then does the thinking: it reads your log and tells you which muscles are recovered, which you are neglecting, where push and pull are out of balance and which lifts have stalled. With ATLAS PRO, the AI planner builds a program around your days, time, equipment and history, and the AI coach answers questions about your own training and proposes exact changes to your plan. You apply them with a tap, and every change can be undone.
